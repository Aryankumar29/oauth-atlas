## Candidate corrections to investigate

These are hypotheses, not verified claims. Confirm before publishing.

- **Google:** not every scope triggers verification; basic scopes, sensitive scopes, and restricted scopes may follow different paths.
- **Slack:** an internal app installed in your own workspace may not need public distribution review; admin approval may still apply.
- **Notion:** internal integrations and public integrations may have different review requirements.
- **GitHub:** OAuth Apps, GitHub Apps, and Marketplace listings are different products with different review paths.
- **HubSpot:** private apps and Marketplace apps may have different review requirements.

## What to capture

- The exact claim that changed.
- The source that changed it (URL, quote, date).
- Whether the change affects time, difficulty, blockers, or the agent/human split.
- The guide version before and after.
